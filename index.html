Privacy Policy for Mind Flip: Reality Break

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use Mind Flip: Reality Break, an Android mobile game.

1. Information We Collect

Mind Flip: Reality Break does not require you to create an account and does not directly ask you to provide personal information such as your name, postal address, or phone number in the game.

The game uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and process information such as IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, and performance information for purposes including advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.

2. Advertising

Mind Flip: Reality Break uses Google AdMob to provide advertisements, including interstitial advertisements displayed at appropriate points such as between game levels.

Depending on your location and applicable privacy requirements, Google may ask for your consent before using information for personalized advertising. You can make or change privacy choices through the consent options presented by the app where applicable.

For more information about how Google handles data, please review Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy .

3. Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK
Used to serve and measure advertisements and support advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention functions.

4. Data Security

We do not directly operate a server that stores personal information submitted through the game. Information processed by third-party services is subject to those services' own security practices and privacy policies.

5. Children's Privacy

Mind Flip: Reality Break is not designed to request personal information directly from users. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the game.

6. Your Privacy Choices

Where required by applicable law, users may be presented with choices regarding the processing of personal data for advertising purposes. Those choices are handled through the consent mechanism provided by Google Mobile Ads and its applicable consent framework.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

For privacy-related questions concerning Mind Flip: Reality Break, please contact the app developer through the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
